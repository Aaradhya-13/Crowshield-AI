The Problem

Crowd safety today is reactive — by design.

CCTV rooms, barricades and manual headcounts only tell you what already happened. A stampede takes seconds to start and minutes to become a tragedy; traditional tools react on the same timescale as the disaster itself.

Reactive Manual CCTV monitoring

Operators scan dozens of feeds by eye. Early warning signs — density spikes, reverse flow, bottlenecks — are missed until crowds are already unstable.

Fragmented Disconnected response

Police, organizers and venue staff act on different information, radioed manually, with no shared live picture of risk across the venue.

Unscalable Hardware-heavy fixes

Sensor-grid or LIDAR solutions are expensive to deploy at the scale of Indian festivals, pilgrimages and stadium events.
